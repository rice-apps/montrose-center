# Workflows

## Directory publishing

An authorized admin edits a service, verifies its details, and publishes it. Visitors can find published services without an account. Track the information owner and last verification date.

## Referrals — proposed, pending staff validation

Submitted → Accepted → Scheduled → Completed.

Staff may also need Waitlisted, Declined, or Cancelled outcomes. Confirm valid transitions, reassignment, required fields, and responsibility at each step with the Center before implementation.

Every referral should have a destination, responsible team, timestamps, and a history of state changes. Do not treat a referral as proof that a service was delivered.

## First demonstration

Publish a service, find it in the public directory, create a fictional referral, and move it through the agreed staff workflow.

# Project brief

Empower Houston's LGBTQ+ community through mental health services, housing support, and community connection.

## MVP

- Public service directory with eligibility, costs, contact details, accessibility, and next steps.
- Staff referral workflow replacing paper handoffs, with clear ownership and status history.
- Administration for keeping service information accurate and published.

## Later scope

Optional Pydantic AI service-finding assistant, booking follow-ups, community events and surveys, and a Summit experience. Confirm the year for the February 7–8 Summit before scheduling work.

## Open decisions

Confirm the Center's record system, required referral data, staff roles, operating budget, and long-term technical owner. This platform is not currently scoped as an EHR replacement.
